## Fingertip joint: measure before tightening

The bracket has approximately 4.6 mm clearance holes on a 21 mm pitch. That
suggests M4 clearance; **it does not prove that the tip has M4 threads**. The
M4 × 12 suggestion is a trial length, not a dimension derived from a verified
assembly. Use it only after confirming the receiving thread or nut arrangement.
There are two screws per tip; one position shares the finger-holder connection,
so the two stacks may require different lengths.

Measure the clamped thickness and available thread depth. The screw must clamp
the entire stack with suitable engagement before bottoming. Do not force an M4
screw into an unknown thread or heat-set an M3 insert into these clearance holes.
For purchased tips, follow the tip’s specified hardware. Rigid printed prototypes
retain source mounting pockets, but their receiving hardware remains unverified.

## Heat-set insert locations

Original specification: M3 thread, nominal 4 mm outside diameter, 5 mm length.
Check actual insert knurl diameter and pocket depth; a supplier’s M3 label alone
does not establish fit.

This is 19 inserts with camera, Quest support and printed stops. Install while
parts are separate. **No insert goes in the pinion bearing recess, carriage
screw counterbores or larger fingertip clearance holes.** Let inserts cool,
then check engagement without forcing a bottomed screw.
